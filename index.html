02 — about

A developer's logic.
A designer's instinct.

Hi, I'm Johannes!

I build things that work — then make them mean something.

I am Johannes Emmanuel A. Claus, a BS Information Technology student at National University (NU) Lipa, specializing in Mobile & Web Applications. I have worked on full-stack development and UI/UX design principles through the Web Systems and mobile applications I have created throughout my IT journey at the university. I am also skilled in project management, as I led my group in completing our Capstone Project courses in previous terms. My tech side taught me how things work, and it helped me understand that backend processes are the most important of all; without proper processes, a system won’t work or will fail.

More than just an IT student, I tend to explore beyond my chosen career path and skills. I also served as a Graphic Design Artist with the NU Lipa Marketing Office for more than two years. I created various marketing and promotional materials for my university. I had a versatile role where I handled both printed and digital materials for every university marketing campaign. This experience opened doors to new opportunities, including my appointment as Creative Director of the NU Lipa School of Architecture, Computing and Engineering Student Council.

Based inBatangas City, PH
