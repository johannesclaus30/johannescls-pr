02 — about

A developer's logic.
A designer's instinct.

Hi, I'm Johannes!

Curious about how it works.
Careful about how it feels.

I’m Johannes Emmanuel A. Claus, a BS Information Technology student at NU Lipa, specializing in Mobile & Web Applications. I build mobile and web systems, explore UI/UX, and enjoy making the logic behind an interface work as well as the interface looks.

As our capstone team’s project lead and web admin developer, I helped organize the work, guide the team through our Capstone Project courses, and develop both the web admin side and mobile app. That experience shaped how I approach a build: understand the process, connect the pieces, and keep the people using it in mind.

My creative work runs alongside my technical work. As a Student Graphic Designer with the NU Lipa Marketing Office, I create print and digital campaign materials. I also serve as Creative Director of the SACE Student Council, bringing visual identity and student stories to life.

Based inBatangas City, PH
CurrentlyIT student & designer
